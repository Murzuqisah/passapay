# Cross-border Payments for Artists — Simplified Overview

## Project Goal

Enable artists performing abroad to receive instant payment and convert it to their home currency without intermediaries, escrow, or holding periods.

## Core Flow

### 1. Payment Process

**When the artist performs:**

- Promoter logs into platform and initiates payment
- Selects artist from verified list or enters wallet address
- Enters payment amount in USD (displays equivalent in local currency)
- Reviews transaction details including fees
- Confirms payment through wallet signature
- Payment in stablecoins (USDC) goes directly to artist's digital wallet

### 2. Instant Access

- Artist receives real-time notification
- Dashboard updates with new balance
- Transaction history shows sender, amount, timestamp
- Full blockchain verification available

### 3. Currency Conversion (Future Phase)

*Note: On-ramping and off-ramping will be implemented in later phases*

- Artist converts earnings to local currency (KES, NGN, etc.)
- Withdraws via M-Pesa or bank transfer
- Conversion time: minutes (M-Pesa) to hours (bank transfer)

## Why This Solution?

### Blockchain Benefits

- **Speed** – Instant cross-border payments regardless of country
- **Low Cost** – Minimal transaction fees vs traditional transfers
- **Transparency** – Secure, traceable blockchain ledger
- **Programmable** – Can automate compliance features
- **Permissionless** – No traditional banking infrastructure required

## Example Scenario

**Kenyan Artist in Nigeria:**

1. Bien performs in Lagos
2. Promoter sends $5,000 in USDC
3. Bien's wallet receives it instantly
4. *(Future)* Converts to KES and withdraws to M-Pesa

## MVP Focus

For the initial version, we focus on:

- Account creation and wallet setup
- Peer-to-peer payment functionality
- Transaction tracking and history
- Smart contract-based transfers
- Basic security features (2FA, wallet signatures)

**Future Enhancements:**

- Fiat on/off-ramp integrations
- Multi-currency support
- Automated tax withholding
- Bulk payment options
- Advanced compliance features
